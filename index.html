02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Too curious
to leave things
alone.

I'm a curious person who tends to get invested in the things that interest me. I rarely stop at just knowing how something works; I want to get into it, experiment, understand what's happening behind the scenes and see if there's a better way. One question usually leads to another, and before I know it, I've gone down a rabbit hole I never planned on exploring. I like having something to work towards, whether it's a project, a new skill or an idea I've been meaning to try. I'm also a little particular about things — I know when something doesn't feel quite right, and I'm happy to rethink it until it does. I enjoy the creative side as much as the logical, and like keeping things simple without making them boring. I don't take everything too seriously either; I enjoy a good challenge, a bit of humour and occasionally getting carried away with an idea. There's probably more I'd like to explore than I have time for, but that's part of the fun.
